Gut Health and Weight Management

A one-on-one plan for nutrition, digestion and sustainable weight loss, built around your schedule, your training and the foods you actually eat.

Book a ConsultCall (817) 420-7696

What Is Gut Health and Weight Management?

Our gut health program is a personalized plan for nutrition, digestion and weight management, with regular check-ins to track progress. It focuses on habits you can keep instead of crash diets.

Extra weight adds load to your back, hips and knees and makes recovering from injury harder. Getting it down is often part of getting out of pain for good.

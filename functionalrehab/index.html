What Is Functional Rehab?

Functional rehab is coached, progressive exercise that rebuilds the strength, mobility and control your body needs for daily life and sport. Each program is matched to what you can't do right now and progressed visit by visit, not handed out as a generic sheet.

Pain relief opens the door. Strength keeps it shut. Stronger muscles absorb force better and give your nervous system confidence, which turns the pain alarm down.

What to Expect

  • Work at a challenging but safe effort, about 7 to 8 out of 10
  • Muscle-working discomfort is fine; real pain means we stop and modify
  • Clear progressions as you get stronger

After Your Visit

  • Do your home program most days
  • Track your sets and how you feel the next day
  • Stay active between visits

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Tell us about any new pain, numbness or weakness
  • Hard-pain, not exercise effort, is a signal to stop that movement

Common Questions

Have another question? Call or text (817) 420-7696.

What's the difference between rehab and an adjustment?

An adjustment reduces pain and restores motion quickly. Rehab builds the strength and control that keep the problem from coming back. Most of our plans use both.

How hard are the rehab sessions?

We aim for about a 7 to 8 out of 10 effort on each set. Hard enough to change something, but never painful in a way that hurts the tissue.

Can I do rehab online?

Yes. Our online programs at RESET (resethybridrecovery.com) follow the same approach for back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip, golf and more.

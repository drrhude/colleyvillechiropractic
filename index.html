Meet Your Chiropractor

Dr. Jon "Rudy" Urrutia, DC

Dr. Rudy served 10 years on active duty in the U.S. Coast Guard, then earned his bachelor's degree and Doctor of Chiropractic from Parker University in Dallas. In 2023 he took over Colleyville Chiropractic, a clinic that has cared for this community since 1999.

His approach is simple: find where your pain is really coming from, calm it down, then build the strength to keep it from coming back. He chooses techniques for the person on the table, from gentle instrument adjustments to hands-on work and coached rehab, and he sees patients in English and Spanish.

"Movement is medicine. Your body is resilient by design. Our job is to give it the way there."

Plan on about an hour. You'll leave knowing what's going on, what we recommend and what it costs, with no pressure to commit.

  1. Book Online

    Choose a time that works in under a minute, or call or text us. Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.

  2. Talk + Exam

    We go over your history and goals, then do an orthopedic, neurological and movement exam.

  3. Your Plan

    You hear what we found in plain English, what we recommend and what it costs. If chiropractic isn't the right fit, we'll tell you.

  4. First Treatment

    When it's appropriate, you're treated the same day and leave with a simple plan for home.

The 35 / 65 Rule

What we do in the office is only part of the picture. The rest of your week matters more.

35%In our office

Outside it: sleep, stress, daily movement and the simple home plan we give you. That's where the change sticks.

FAQs

New Patient Questions

Quick answers to what people ask most before their first visit.

See All FAQs
What happens at my first visit?

We talk through your history and what you want to get back to, then do an orthopedic, neurological and movement exam. You'll hear what we found in plain English and what we recommend. When it's appropriate, you get your first treatment the same day. Plan on about an hour.

How much is the first visit?

New patients can start with our $47 new patient special. A standard new patient exam is $120. If you're using Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, we'll verify your benefits first.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. We're in-network with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas. We verify your benefits and eligibility before your visit so you know what to expect. For other plans, call us and we'll walk through your options.

Do I need a referral to see a chiropractor?

No. You can book directly with us online or by calling or texting (817) 420-7696.

Do adjustments hurt?

Most people feel relief, not pain. Mild soreness for 6 to 24 hours is normal, like after a workout. If you'd rather avoid the popping, we use gentler options like the Activator instrument or a drop table.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on and how long it's been there. Most plans move through three phases: get you out of pain, restore good movement, then reinforce it with strength. You'll get a clear recommendation after your exam, with no pressure to commit up front.

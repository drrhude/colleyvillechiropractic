The people you'll see

A small team, so you see the same faces every visit. This clinic has cared for Colleyville families for 25 years, and we treat everyone who walks in like a neighbor.

Photo: Dr. Rudy portrait

Dr. Jon "Rudy" Urrutia, DC

Owner and chiropractor

Dr. Rudy is a U.S. Coast Guard veteran and the owner of Colleyville Chiropractic | CVC Spine + Sport. His time in the service shaped his belief in discipline, resilience and purpose, and he brings those to every patient.

His approach combines chiropractic adjustments, shockwave therapy and movement-based rehab to fix the cause of pain, then build the strength to keep it away. He treats everyone from kids and expecting moms to athletes and grandparents, and he sees patients in English and Spanish.

Photo: Payton portrait

Payton Lapp

Clinic manager

Payton is the friendly face that greets you when you walk in. She runs the front of the clinic, from scheduling and insurance questions to helping during your care.

If you have a question about your appointment, your benefits or your bill, she's your first call.

Photo: Arwen portrait

Arwen Earll Boyer, LMT

Licensed massage therapist

Arwen brings over 20 years of experience in deep tissue, myofascial release, prenatal and postpartum massage, sports recovery and relaxation work.

She tailors every session to what you need that day and works alongside your chiropractic and rehab care when it helps.

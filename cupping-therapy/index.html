What Is Cupping Therapy?

Cupping therapy uses cups that create gentle suction to lift the skin and underlying tissue. This decompresses tight muscle and draws blood flow to the area to ease tension and support recovery. The round marks it leaves are normal, painless and fade within about a week.

We use cupping as a comfort and recovery tool alongside active care, not as a stand-alone fix.

What to Expect

  • A pulling or tugging sensation that most people find relaxing
  • Round marks that fade in a few days to a week
  • A looser feeling afterward

After Your Visit

  • Stay hydrated
  • Do your mobility work while the tissue feels looser
  • Keep the area warm; gentle movement is good

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Not over broken skin, rashes or recent injuries
  • Tell us if you bruise easily or take blood thinners; we adjust suction
  • Skip very hot baths or saunas right after
What the research says. Cupping may offer short-term relief of muscle pain and tightness. The quality of evidence is limited and mixed, so we use it as a comfort and recovery adjunct alongside active care.

Do cupping marks hurt?

No. The marks are discoloration from increased blood flow, not injury. They don't hurt and fade within a few days to a week.

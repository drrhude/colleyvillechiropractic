What Is Prenatal Chiropractic?

Prenatal chiropractic is chiropractic care adapted for pregnancy. As your body changes, your posture, pelvis and low back take on new load. We use pregnancy-friendly positioning and gentler techniques to ease back, hip and pelvic pain, alongside simple exercises that help you stay comfortable and active.

We work alongside your OB or midwife, and we'll always ask about your pregnancy before we treat.

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Bleeding, fluid leakage, severe headache, sudden swelling or contractions need your OB or midwife right away
  • Let us know about any high-risk pregnancy factors
  • Shockwave and some therapies aren't used during pregnancy
What the research says. Back and pelvic pain are common in pregnancy, and research supports exercise and manual care as reasonable options for many women. We tailor care to you and coordinate with your OB or midwife.

Common Questions

Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy?

For most uncomplicated pregnancies, yes, with adapted positioning and gentler techniques. We ask about your pregnancy first, coordinate with your OB or midwife, and avoid therapies like shockwave.

Can you help with sciatica during pregnancy?

Often, yes. Sciatica and pelvic pain are some of the most common reasons expecting moms see us.

What Is Myofascial Release?

Myofascial release is sustained, targeted pressure and stretching of muscle and fascia, the connective tissue layer around muscle. It releases tight, restricted areas and improves how tissue glides, which often makes movement noticeably easier.

The gains in motion last longer when we follow them with exercise, so we pair release work with mobility and strength.

What It Helps With

Your exam tells us whether myofascial release is the right fit for you.

  • Muscle tightness and knots
  • Movement restrictions
  • Trigger points
  • Tension headaches from the neck and shoulders
  • Post-injury guarding

What to Expect

  • Firm pressure that can feel good-sore in tight spots
  • Mild soreness for up to a day, like a deep massage
  • Easier movement afterward

After Your Visit

  • Drink water
  • Move gently through your new range and do your mobility work
  • Use heat afterward if it feels good (15 to 20 minutes)

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Avoid hard workouts of the area the rest of the day if it's sore
  • Don't stretch aggressively into pain
  • Tell us if bruising or sharp pain develops
What the research says. Manual soft-tissue work may reduce pain and improve short-term mobility. It's most effective combined with active exercise that reinforces the new motion.

How is myofascial release different from massage?

It's more targeted: sustained pressure and stretching on specific restricted areas, usually as part of a treatment plan, while massage covers broader areas and longer sessions.

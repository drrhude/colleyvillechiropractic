Your first visit

What happens at my first visit?

We talk through your history and what hurts, do an exam and movement screen, and explain what we find in plain English. When it's appropriate, you get your first treatment the same day. Plan on about an hour.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with us.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like athletic wear. We may have you do some simple movements during the exam.

Do you see kids, expecting moms and seniors?

Yes. We're a whole-family practice. We use gentle, low-force techniques when they're the better fit, and we adapt positioning for pregnancy.

Insurance and cost

Do you take insurance?

We're in-network with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas and can verify your benefits by phone before your visit. For other plans, call us and we'll explain your options.

How much does a visit cost without insurance?

A new patient visit is $99, or $47 with our new patient special. Standard chiropractic visits are $60, or $45 with a membership. Full prices are on our pricing page.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Yes, we accept HSA and FSA cards.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through CareCredit and Cherry for larger programs like NeuroPath.

Treatment

Do adjustments hurt?

Most people feel relief, not pain. If you'd rather avoid the popping, we use gentler options like the Activator instrument or a drop table.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on and how long it's been there. We'll give you a clear recommendation after your exam, and we re-check your progress along the way. There's no pressure to commit to a long plan up front.

What is shockwave therapy?

Radial shockwave sends pressure waves into stubborn tendon and soft-tissue pain, like plantar fasciitis or tennis elbow. Sessions take about 15 minutes, with no needles or downtime.

Can I book a massage without seeing the chiropractor?

Yes. Massage can be booked on its own or as part of your care plan.

Do you offer online programs?

Yes. RESET, our online rehab and training brand, has programs for back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip, golf and more at resethybridrecovery.com.

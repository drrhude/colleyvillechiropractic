Getting Started

What happens at my first visit?

We talk through your history and what you want to get back to, then do an orthopedic, neurological and movement exam. You'll hear what we found in plain English and what we recommend. When it's appropriate, you get your first treatment the same day. Plan on about an hour.

Do I need a referral to see a chiropractor?

No. You can book directly with us online or by calling or texting (817) 420-7696.

How much is the first visit?

New patients can start with our $47 new patient special. A standard new patient exam is $120. If you're using Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, we'll verify your benefits first.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like athletic wear. We'll have you do some simple movements during the exam.

Chiropractic Care

Do adjustments hurt?

Most people feel relief, not pain. Mild soreness for 6 to 24 hours is normal, like after a workout. If you'd rather avoid the popping, we use gentler options like the Activator instrument or a drop table.

What is the popping sound during an adjustment?

It's gas releasing from the joint, not bones cracking. Not every adjustment makes a sound, and the sound isn't what makes it work.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on and how long it's been there. Most plans move through three phases: get you out of pain, restore good movement, then reinforce it with strength. You'll get a clear recommendation after your exam, with no pressure to commit up front.

Why do you give exercises?

Adjustments reduce pain and restore motion, but they don't make you stronger. Strength is what keeps pain from coming back. About 35% of your recovery happens in our office; the other 65% is how you move, sleep and live between visits.

Is chiropractic safe?

For most people, yes. Serious complications are rare. We screen your health history first, choose techniques for your age and condition, and refer you to another provider when something isn't right for chiropractic care.

Shockwave Therapy

What is shockwave therapy?

Shockwave therapy (ESWT) sends acoustic pressure waves into a painful tendon or soft tissue to increase blood flow and trigger healing. It's used for plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, Achilles and shoulder tendon pain, and other stubborn soft-tissue problems.

How many shockwave sessions will I need?

Most plans are 5 to 10 sessions, once a week.

How much is shockwave therapy?

A single session is $149. The 5-pack is $110 per session ($550) and the 10-pack is $99 per session ($990). Members pay $79 per session.

Can I take ibuprofen after shockwave?

We recommend skipping ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin and ice around treatment days. Shockwave works by triggering a controlled healing response, and anti-inflammatories blunt it. Acetaminophen is usually fine; confirm with your provider or pharmacist.

Who shouldn't get shockwave?

We don't use it during pregnancy, over cancer, infection or open wounds, or near a pacemaker. Tell us about blood thinners or a recent steroid or PRP injection.

Other Treatments

What is NeuroPath?

NeuroPath is our structured program for numbness, tingling, burning and balance problems in the feet and hands. It starts with testing, then combines in-office treatment, home devices and rehab with regular re-testing.

Can I book a massage without seeing the chiropractor?

Yes. Massage with our licensed massage therapist can be booked on its own or alongside your care.

Do the cupping marks hurt?

No. They're discoloration from increased blood flow, not injury, and they fade within a few days to a week.

Is red light therapy safe?

Yes for most people. It uses no UV and doesn't damage skin. Don't look directly into the light, and tell us if you take a photosensitizing medication.

Do you offer online programs?

Yes. RESET, our online rehab and training brand, has programs for back, neck, shoulder, knee, hip, golf and more at resethybridrecovery.com.

Kids, Pregnancy + Seniors

Do you see kids?

Yes. We use gentle, low-force techniques sized for children and teens, and a parent is always present. We focus on musculoskeletal concerns like posture, sports strains and back or neck pain.

Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy?

For most uncomplicated pregnancies, yes, with pregnancy-friendly positioning and gentler techniques. We coordinate with your OB or midwife and don't use shockwave during pregnancy.

Is chiropractic safe for seniors?

Yes, with the right technique. We screen for bone health and medications and use gentle, low-force methods when they're the better fit, plus balance work to help reduce fall risk.

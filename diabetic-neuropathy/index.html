What Is Diabetic Neuropathy?

Diabetic peripheral neuropathy is nerve damage caused by high blood sugar over time. It usually starts in the toes and feet and moves upward, causing numbness, tingling, burning or sharp pain, and it can make balance worse and injuries easier to miss.

Blood sugar control is the foundation, and that stays with your medical team. NeuroPath adds what usually gets left out: measuring sensation and balance, daily home light therapy and nerve stimulation, nutrition support, and strength and balance work so you feel steadier on your feet.

Because numb feet can hide cuts and blisters, we check your feet at every visit and teach you a daily self-check.

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • An open wound or ulcer on the foot needs medical care first
  • A pacemaker or implanted electronic device
  • Pregnancy
  • Sudden weakness, a foot that's hot, red and swollen, or rapidly worsening symptoms need medical evaluation now
What the research says. Good blood sugar control and regular foot care are the best-supported ways to slow diabetic neuropathy. Research on infrared light and electrical stimulation is mixed, and balance training reliably reduces fall risk. We track your results so you know whether the plan is working.

Common Questions

Have another question? Call or text (817) 420-7696.

Can diabetic neuropathy be reversed?

Some nerve damage is permanent. Many people still improve their symptoms, sensation and balance, especially with better blood sugar control and consistent care. We'll tell you honestly what to expect after testing.

Do I need to stop my neuropathy medication?

No. Never change a medication without your prescribing doctor. NeuroPath works alongside your medical care.

Why is balance part of a neuropathy program?

When your feet can't feel the ground well, your balance suffers and fall risk goes up. Training balance and strength is one of the most reliable ways to stay safe and independent.

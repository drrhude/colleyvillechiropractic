What Is Pediatric Chiropractic?

Pediatric chiropractic is chiropractic care adapted for children and teens, using gentle, low-force techniques like the Activator instrument and much lighter pressure than adult care. We focus on musculoskeletal issues like posture, sports strains and back or neck pain, and we refer to your pediatrician when something needs medical care.

For young athletes and dancers, we pair care with age-appropriate strength and control work so they build a body that holds up to their sport.

Is chiropractic safe for kids?

Gentle, low-force chiropractic care for musculoskeletal problems is generally well tolerated in children. We use lighter techniques, a parent is always present, and we refer to your pediatrician when something needs medical care.

What age do you start seeing kids?

We see children and teens of all ages for musculoskeletal concerns. Call us and we'll talk through your child's situation.

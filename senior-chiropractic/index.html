What Is Senior Chiropractic Care?

Chiropractic care for seniors uses gentle, low-force techniques and adjusted pressure to ease back pain, stiffness and arthritis-related aches. We pair it with balance and strength work, because staying strong is one of the best ways to stay independent and reduce fall risk.

We review your health history and bone health before choosing a technique, and we never use more force than you need.

Reviewed by Dr. Jon "Rudy" Urrutia, DC
Updated October 2026

What It Helps With

Your exam tells us whether this care is the right fit for you.

  • Arthritis pain
  • Low back pain
  • Neck stiffness
  • Spinal stenosis symptoms
  • Balance and fall risk
  • Hip and knee pain
  • Neuropathy

What to Expect

  • A careful history, including bone health and medications
  • Gentle techniques like the Activator or drop table
  • Simple strength and balance exercises

After Your Visit

  • Stay active daily
  • Do your balance exercises
  • Tell us about new symptoms or medication changes

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Osteoporosis changes which techniques we use
  • Blood thinners affect some therapies
  • New numbness, weakness or bladder changes need medical evaluation
What the research says. Strength and balance training reduce fall risk in older adults, and gentle manual care with exercise is a reasonable option for back and joint pain.

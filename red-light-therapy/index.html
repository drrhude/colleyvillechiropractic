What Is Red Light Therapy?

Red light therapy, or photobiomodulation, uses red and near-infrared light that your cells absorb. It may boost local energy production, support tissue repair and reduce inflammatory signaling. It doesn't damage skin the way UV does, and most people feel only gentle warmth.

Results build with consistent sessions, so we use it as part of a plan alongside exercise and manual care.

Common Questions

Is red light therapy safe?

Yes for most people. It uses no UV and doesn't damage skin. Don't look directly into the light, and tell us if you take a photosensitizing medication.

How often should I do red light therapy?

Effects build with consistent sessions. We'll recommend a frequency based on your goal, and we pair it with your active care.

Dr. Jon "Rudy" Urrutia, DC

Owner and Chiropractor

Dr. Rudy is a U.S. Coast Guard veteran and the owner of Colleyville Chiropractic | CVC Spine. He took over the practice in 2023, carrying forward a clinic that has served Colleyville for 25 years. His time in the service shaped his belief in discipline, resilience and purpose, and he brings those to every patient.

His approach is simple: find the real source of your pain, get you out of it fast, then build the strength to keep it gone. He combines chiropractic adjustments, shockwave therapy, soft-tissue work and coached rehab, and he chooses techniques for the person on the table, from gentle instrument work for kids and seniors to hands-on adjustments for athletes.

He also built NeuroPath, the clinic's program for neuropathy symptoms, and RESET, an online rehab and training brand that brings the same approach to people outside Colleyville. He sees patients in English and Spanish.

"Movement is medicine. Your body is resilient by design. It wants to heal and be strong, and our job is to give it the way there."

Website Privacy

A plain-English summary of what this website collects and where it goes.

What This Site Collects

This website doesn't ask for your health history. Please don't send medical details through any form or text message on this site.

Online Booking

Booking buttons take you to our scheduling system, Jane (colleyvillechiropractic.janeapp.com). Information you enter there is handled under Jane's own privacy and security practices.

Forms

If you use a form on this site, such as the partner form, we receive the contact details and notes you type so we can reply to you. We don't sell this information.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use, so we can improve the site. It uses cookies and doesn't tell us who you are.

Your Health Records

Your patient records are protected under HIPAA and handled under our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is available at the front desk or by request.

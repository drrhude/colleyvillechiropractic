Does this sound familiar?

  • Numbness or pins and needles in your feet or hands
  • Burning or electric pain, often worse at night
  • Feet that feel cold, heavy or like you're walking on cotton
  • Losing your balance more than you used to
  • You've been told to "just live with it"

Nerve symptoms have many causes, including diabetes, circulation, chemotherapy, and pressure on nerves in the spine. NeuroPath is designed to work alongside your medical care, not replace it.

How the program works

Every plan starts with measurements, so you and we can see whether it's working.

Test

Sensation, balance and circulation checks, plus a review of your history and medications.

Plan

A program matched to your results, explained clearly, including the cost, before you commit.

Treat

In-office visits combining light therapy, nerve stimulation, rehab and balance work.

At home

Home devices and a daily routine between visits, with regular re-testing.

What a plan can include

Not every patient needs every piece. We recommend what fits your findings.

Light therapy

Infrared light therapy used in the office and with a home device between visits.

Nerve stimulation

Gentle electrical stimulation for the feet and legs.

Balance and rehab

Vibration plate, compression and exercises to improve stability and reduce fall risk.

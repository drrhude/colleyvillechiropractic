What Is Chiropractic Care?

A chiropractic adjustment is a quick, controlled force applied to a joint that isn't moving well, most often in the spine. It can reduce pain and muscle guarding and restore motion. The pop you may hear is gas releasing from the joint, not bones cracking.

An adjustment opens a window: less pain, more motion. What it doesn't do on its own is make you stronger. That's why most of our plans pair adjustments with targeted exercise, so you're not back on the table every few weeks with the same problem.

Prefer no popping? We use three techniques and pick the one you're comfortable with: Diversified (classic hands-on), Thompson (a drop table that uses less force) and Activator (a gentle handheld instrument). Dr. Rudy is Advanced Activator certified.

What to Expect

  • You may feel looser and more mobile right away
  • Mild soreness for 6 to 24 hours is normal, like after a workout
  • Results usually build over a series of visits, not just one

After Your Visit

  • Move normally; gentle activity helps the adjustment hold
  • Do your assigned home exercises
  • Heat for muscle tension, ice for sharp new soreness (15 minutes)

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Don't force or self-crack your neck or back
  • Skip heavy lifting or intense new activity the first day
  • Sharp, worsening or radiating pain, numbness or weakness is not normal. Call us.
What the research says. Spinal manipulation is a recommended option for low back pain in clinical guidelines, and it works best combined with exercise and staying active.

Common Questions

Have another question? Call or text (817) 420-7696.

Does a chiropractic adjustment hurt?

Most people feel relief, not pain. Some feel mild soreness for a day, like after a workout. If you'd rather avoid the popping, we use gentler options like the Activator instrument or a drop table.

How many chiropractic visits will I need?

It depends on what's going on and how long it's been there. You'll get a clear recommendation after your exam, and we re-check your progress along the way. There's no pressure to commit to a long plan up front.

Do you take insurance for chiropractic?

Yes. We're in-network with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas and can verify your benefits before your first visit. Self-pay visits are $60, or $45 with a membership.

What happens at my first chiropractic visit?

We talk through your history, do an orthopedic, neurological and movement exam, and explain what we find in plain English. When it's appropriate, you get your first treatment the same day. Plan on about an hour.

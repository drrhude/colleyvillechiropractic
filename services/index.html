What an adjustment actually does

An adjustment is a quick, controlled movement to a joint that isn't moving well. It can reduce pain and muscle guarding and make it easier to move, which is often what you need to get back to normal activity.

What it doesn't do on its own is make you stronger. That's why most of our plans pair adjustments with targeted exercise, so you're not back on the table every few weeks with the same problem.

  • Diversified: classic hands-on adjustments
  • Thompson: a drop table that uses less force
  • Activator: a gentle handheld instrument, no twisting or popping

Prefer no popping? Tell us. We'll pick the technique you're comfortable with.

What Is Massage Therapy?

Massage therapy is hands-on work on muscles and connective tissue to ease tension, reduce soreness and help you recover. Sessions are tailored to your goal that day, from focused deep tissue work on a stubborn area to full-body relaxation.

Massage can be booked on its own, without a chiropractic visit, or used alongside your care plan.

Is It Right for You?

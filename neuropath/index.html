What Is NeuroPath?

NeuroPath is our program for peripheral neuropathy symptoms like numbness, tingling, burning and poor balance in the feet and hands. It starts with sensation, balance and circulation testing, then combines in-office treatment, home devices and rehab, with regular re-testing to track change.

Nerve symptoms have many causes, including diabetes, circulation problems, chemotherapy and pressure on nerves in the spine. NeuroPath is designed to work alongside your medical care, not replace it.

Is It Right for You?

Tell us before treatment if any of these apply:

  • Tell us about a pacemaker or implanted electronic device
  • Tell us if you are or could be pregnant
  • New, sudden or rapidly worsening symptoms need medical evaluation first

Common Questions

Can neuropathy be treated without medication?

Many people improve their symptoms and balance with a combination of light therapy, nerve stimulation and targeted exercise. Results vary by cause and how long symptoms have been present, so we test first and tell you honestly whether you're a good fit.

What happens at a NeuroPath consult?

We review your history and medications and test sensation, balance and circulation in your feet and hands. Then we explain what we found and whether NeuroPath fits.

Does insurance cover NeuroPath?
